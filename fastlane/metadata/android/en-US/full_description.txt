VantaCut Studio is a cinematic video editor for Android, built on the open-source FilmCraft engine. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no cloud, no ads.

Edit
* Start from your photo library, from files or blank; open videos shared from other apps
* Trim, move, split, delete, duplicate and replace clips; undo and redo
* Speed from 0.1x to 10x (the sound keeps its pitch), freeze frames, volume, fades, extract audio
* Record a voiceover

Look
* 37 cinematic filters and 23 effects, opacity, mirror and flip; move, scale and turn clips on the preview
* 50 transitions, intros and outros
* Text with presets, the phone's fonts, colours and layout
* 11 aspect ratios and blank clips

Captions and speech
* Captions made on the phone by a speech model, then edited, styled and burned into the video
* Import caption files (SRT, WebVTT, TTML, SCC, MCC, EBU STL) and export SRT sidecars
* Six caption looks, from broadcast to VantaCut's violet
* Arabic and mixed Arabic/English captions, shaped and laid out right-to-left
* Text to speech with two built-in voices and nine natural voices
* The speech models are downloaded once, only when you ask; the sound never leaves the phone

Export
* 720p, 1080p or 4K at 24 to 60 fps, saved to your Movies folder and ready to share

Works upright and sideways. VantaCut Studio is in beta.

VantaCut Studio is built on FilmCraft (MIT OR Apache-2.0), an open-source reimplementation of Adobe Premiere in Rust with a Kotlin UI. VantaCut Studio is not affiliated with or endorsed by Adobe. Premiere is a trademark of Adobe Inc.
